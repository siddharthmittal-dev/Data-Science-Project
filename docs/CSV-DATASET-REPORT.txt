Forecast definitions
Busyness means completed visits per calendar day. No-show volume means missed
non-cancelled visits per day. These match the definitions in the agreed dataset
plan. Predictions are constrained to non-negative published daily capacity.
Individual no-show features use the information available at booking time.
Cancelled appointments are excluded from individual no-show outcomes.

Evaluation protocol
May-August fits are assessed on September for model selection. October is the
holdout month, after refitting on earlier observed data. Daily count forecasts
freeze the available history at the beginning of the target month. Individual
models train only on outcomes recorded before the target month and calculate
each appointment's features from events recorded before its booking time.
Model selection uses September results. October results do not select the model.

Retention
The requested target remains another completed visit within 90 days.
Monthly cutoffs in June, July and August have complete 90-day follow-up.
September and October cutoffs are censored, with return labels left unknown.
Allowing an independent 90-day holdout leaves a latest training cutoff of
5 May 2026, when only 2 patients have prior completed visits. This supplies
too little training evidence for a meaningful independent retention backtest.
Additional earlier history or subsequent follow-up is required.
