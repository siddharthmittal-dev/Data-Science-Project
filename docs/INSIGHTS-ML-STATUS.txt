Terra Insights — current model status
26 November 2026

Current appointment aggregates and their weekday baseline are separate from trained
workload datasets. They must not be summed or described as the same population.

The uploaded CSV report covers 376 appointments and 122 patients in May-October
2026, with planned capacity through November. OLS count models forecast completed
visits and no-show counts. Insights shows coefficients, worked daily contributions,
October actual/predicted and residual/scatter plots, MAE, RMSE and R-squared.
Individual no-show logistic evaluation shows ROC/AUC, precision-recall/AP,
calibration, confusion matrix, coefficients and standardization.

Recorded November estimates: 51.07 completed visits and 6.02 no-shows.
October individual no-show AUC was 0.4267 with four positive outcomes. Its Brier
score underperformed the baseline; this limitation remains visible.

The separate workload artifact has its own training history and rolling evaluation.
The separate follow-up artifact uses generated patient histories. Source metadata
is retained. These results do not establish performance at an operating clinic.
OLS uncertainty now uses a reproducible 400-draw residual block bootstrap (seed 42,
seven-day blocks aligned to weekdays). Insights shows coefficient confidence ranges,
daily fitted-mean confidence and outcome prediction ranges, monthly prediction
ranges, exact tables and a graph. Nominal 95% coverage is approximate and has not
been validated on future observations. Features and capacity are treated as fixed.
November regression total ranges: completed visits 39.1194-62.1772; no-shows
3.3121-12.3207. Monthly bounds use sums within each bootstrap draw. Existing point
forecasts, selected methods and held-out metrics are unchanged.

The uploaded dataset's independent 90-day retention evaluation remains incomplete:
additional mature history and a retention fitting/evaluation implementation are
needed. See RETENTION-HISTORY-DATASET-PROMPT.txt and CSV-DATASET-REPORT.txt.
